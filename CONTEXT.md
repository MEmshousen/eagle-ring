# Eagle Ring

A webring for Houston City College CS students and alumni: a public directory of members' personal sites, plus an optional widget members embed to link their sites into a ring.

## Language

**School**:
Houston City College (hccs.edu). The institution whose students and alumni the ring serves.
_Avoid_: Houston Community College, HCC, HCCS

**Webring**:
The whole project: the Directory together with the Ring Widget.
_Avoid_: link list, link page

**Directory**:
The public page listing every Member, grouped by Graduation Year.
_Avoid_: index, list, roster

**Ring Widget**:
A small embeddable snippet a Member can add to their own Site that links to the previous, next, and a random Member's Site.
_Avoid_: ring, nav, badge

**Member**:
A current CS student or CS alumnus of the School whose Site is listed in the Directory.
_Avoid_: user, entry, participant

**Site**:
A Member's personal website, the thing the Directory links to.
_Avoid_: portfolio, homepage, link

**Graduation Year**:
The year a Member graduated or expects to graduate from the School; the Directory's grouping key.
_Avoid_: class, cohort, year

**Maintainer**:
The person who reviews and merges Member submissions. A single person for now.
_Avoid_: admin, owner, moderator

**Submission**:
A pull request that adds or edits one Member's record; the only way into the Directory.
_Avoid_: application, request, signup
